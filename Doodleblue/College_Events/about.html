About Campus Events

Campus Events is a student-centric platform that organises and promotes a wide range of events, including technical, cultural, sports and academic activities. Our goal is to encourage participation, creativity and collaboration among students.

Our Mission

Connect students across departments and interests.

Organise meaningful events and experiences throughout the year.
